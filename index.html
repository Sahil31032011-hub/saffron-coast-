Before you drive

How to play

Your goal

Drive around Saffron Coast. Take jobs from the orange rings, earn money, then spend it in the Garage on cars, paint and upgrades. Keep an eye on your fuel.

Keyboard

S / ↓ brake and reverse
A D / ← → steer
Space handbrake drift
Shift nitro
B horn
H headlights
N full map
G garage
C camera view

Phone or tablet

Use the on-screen wheel to steer and the GAS and BRAKE buttons. DRIFT, NOS, LIGHT and HORN are the small buttons. Tap Menu for the Garage, Map and other options. You can move and resize every button.

Rules you must follow

  • Do not run over pedestrians. The police will jail you.
  • Crashing into traffic gives you wanted stars. Lose the police or you get busted.
  • Stay out of the water. If you drive in, reverse out fast.
  • Slow down at the railway crossings when the lights flash.
  • Refuel at a green PETROL ring before the tank runs empty.
